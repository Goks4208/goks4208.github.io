Privacy Policy

Homebridge Nest is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the status of the owner's Nest devices, control them, and view camera streams.

This data is processed only on the owner's own Homebridge server at home. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other person is collected.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's third-party connections settings.
